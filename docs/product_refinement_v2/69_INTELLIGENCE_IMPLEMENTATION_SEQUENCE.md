# Family Intelligence Implementation Sequence — Gate G3

> **Status:** Build-ready roadmap; Backend, provider, remote retrieval, worker, notification and Native implementation remain unauthorized.
> **Goal:** Build explainable intelligence as closed, source-qualified family loops on Render—not as a collection of local advisor screens, hard-coded reports, or generic chatbot promises.
>
> **Superseded (owner decision 2026-10-10):** the "unauthorized" line above governed the foundation wave. The owner's approved SAFETY phase authorizes the backend/native/notification work its slices require (see `docs/safety_phase/01_SAFETY_PHASE_PLAN.md`); education-related intelligence waits for the safety phase to complete (5/5) per the same decision. Historical text kept as written.

## 1. Shared foundation

Before any user-facing intelligence claim, establish the platform primitives that do not themselves require a model:

- Render family/role/child authorization, purpose/consent/visibility and retention policy enforcement;
- versioned source-fact event contracts from Security, Learning and Family, including provenance, quality/freshness and transactional outbox/replay;
- durable evidence-reference/knowledge-context, correction/forget, audit and support design;
- intelligence policy/capability registry, feature gates, evaluation/change-control and notification orchestration;
- shared insight/report/answer states, Design System language and truthful Flutter local/offline states; and
- Firebase auxiliary-service approval records before any FCM use.

Source domains must expose real Render-authoritative events first. Product Ready documentation for Security, Learning and Family Connection is not evidence that their backend/native event producers already exist.

## 2. Proposed vertical sequence

### Slice 1 — Source-qualified event and evidence foundation

A real supported Security, Learning or Family domain event is authorized, accepted once, quality-labelled, stored/referenced durably, appears in its proper domain timeline, and can be inspected by the entitled guardian with source/freshness/visibility truth.

**Why first:** It creates the foundation for every later intelligence capability without pretending to infer anything.

### Slice 2 — Explainable deterministic signal and guardian review loop

A narrow, low-impact, versioned deterministic rule receives sufficient approved source facts, creates an explainable signal/insight, shows its evidence/limits, and lets a guardian support, defer, dismiss, or correct it. The linked Safety/Learning/Family action proves its own independent lifecycle.

**Why next:** It validates human decision support, policy enforcement, feedback and audit before patterns or a model provider.

### Slice 3 — Knowledge context, correction and forget loop

An authorized guardian inspects purpose/source/visibility for a contextual item, adds correction context or submits a forget/delete request, sees eligible downstream propagation, and receives a durable partial/completed/retention-limited result.

**Why next:** Trust, privacy and data lifecycle must precede broad retrieval or reports.

### Slice 4 — Weekly report with honest coverage

A scheduled or manual report collects only authorized evidence, returns ready/partial/insufficient truth, presents one proportionate recommendation, respects audience/notification settings, and records guardian feedback/decision.

**Why next:** It creates recurring family value using the already proven source and review loop without needing natural-language AI.

### Slice 5 — Capability-gated pattern and anomaly loop

After adequate real data history and evaluation, a bounded domain pattern produces a source/coverage/confidence/limitation explanation, supports suppress/correct/retire feedback, and is continuously evaluated for false positives and harmful effects.

**Why later:** A baseline requires time, verified events and calibration. It cannot be simulated from early or sparse families.

### Slice 6 — Role-scoped assistant retrieval loop

After explicit provider/cost/privacy/quality approval, an authorized guardian asks a supported question. Render retrieves only permitted evidence, delivers a cited answer/clarification/“I do not know”/unavailable state, preserves controlled audit, and supports answer feedback.

**Why later:** Natural-language interaction adds provider, injection, privacy, cost and reliability risk; it must consume the proven evidence/memory substrate rather than bypass it.

### Slice 7 — Specialised intelligence capabilities

Voice conversation, sensitive-image analysis, rule updates, Studio generation, Quran recitation analysis and other modality-specific capabilities each progress only through a dedicated input, accuracy, privacy, consent, locale, retention, cost, human-review and recovery decision.

**Why later:** These are distinct programmes, not toggles on a common “AI” switch.

### Slice 8 — Delegated agent decision gate

Consider a narrow, reversible command only after source, policy, insights, reports, retrieval, audit, notifications, underlying domain outcomes and correction/forget controls are operating reliably. Create an explicit Owner decision before enabling any real delegated action.

**Why last:** Delegation magnifies every authorization, reliability and trust failure. It is not an initial value-delivery mechanism.

## 3. Definition of done per slice

Each slice needs all applicable evidence below before it can be considered a real product capability:

1. Render-authoritative family/role/purpose authorization and durable state.
2. Source lineage, quality/freshness/visibility/retention truth and independent domain ownership.
3. No hard-coded product result, simulated success receipt or unlabelled sample family data.
4. Guardian/co-guardian/child journey and protected non-visibility/age-appropriate consequence where relevant.
5. Explainability, insufficiency/partial/unavailable state, feedback/correction/defer/dismiss path and durable audit.
6. Idempotency, retry/replay, event ordering/revocation/expiry, offline/local cache honesty and recovery behavior.
7. Accessibility, Arabic-first RTL/LTR, localisation, large text/reduced motion and non-colour status cues.
8. Privacy/security, abuse/injection/rate-limit testing, support reconstruction, observability and rollback/kill-switch readiness.
9. Provider/model/configuration evaluation, cost/terms/DPA and change-control proof whenever any external AI service is used.
10. Independent verification of any linked Safety, Learning or Family action; assistant/insight state is never the action’s receipt.

## 4. Deferred scope and sequencing guardrails

- No generic “ask anything about my family” launch scope.
- No ambient monitoring, unrestricted private-content ingestion, public child comparison, behavioural scoring, diagnosis or persuasion loop.
- No model-generated report/answer treated as a source fact.
- No direct Flutter provider integration or Firebase system-of-record/AI authority.
- No voice, image, recitation, content generation or delegated action claim until its own capability programme is complete.
- No delegation of high-impact Safety, access, contact, location, learning assessment, discipline, reward, spending or irreversible action.
- No migration from a prototype/local SQLite/mock adapter to production without source, runtime, retention, authorization and audit proof.

## 5. Authorization boundary

This sequence specifies how a future team can build real Family Intelligence using Render and only limited approved Firebase assistance. It does not authorize starting Backend services, data stores, providers, model APIs, workers, FCM, native input capture, remote sync, delegated execution, beta distribution or production release. A separate execution authorization remains required.
