# Family Connection — Phase Initiation

> **Loop card:** CONNECTION-G1-DISCOVERY
> **Status:** Active; G1 direction accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Goal:** Make Family OS the calm, safe coordination layer for family connection—not a generic messenger, a surveillance network, or unrelated calendar/task tools.

## 1. Product outcome

Family Connection helps a family stay in touch, coordinate the day, share safely, and know when someone arrived or needs help.

| Person | Essential question |
|---|---|
| Child | “Can I reach my family, share safely, know what I need to do, and ask for approved connection?” |
| Primary guardian | “Can I coordinate the family, keep contact boundaries clear, and respond without chasing multiple apps?” |
| Co-guardian | “Do I see the same relevant plans, requests, and family communication within my permission?” |
| Family platform | “How do communication, tasks, calendar, safety, learning, location, alerts, and roles reinforce one family rhythm?” |

## 2. The Family Connection Loop

```text
Family member communicates, creates a plan, assigns a responsibility,
or shares a check-in
   → the right person receives a truthful state and useful context
   → they respond, complete, acknowledge, or ask for help
   → family calendar/task/location/safety state is updated
   → Today shows only the relevant outcome or next action
   → the family can review, correct, or recover without losing context
```

A sent message, task, location request, or calendar event is not considered complete until its relevant delivery, acknowledgement, completion, or recovery state is clear.

## 3. Registered connection universe

The active Family Connection pillar contains 7 systems and 39 registered services:

1. Conversations.
2. Calls.
3. Media and files.
4. Safe contact circle.
5. Family calendar.
6. Tasks and responsibilities.
7. Location in communication.

Calendar and tasks are not Future Developments: they are registered current systems. Advanced household planning such as shopping, recipes, or meal planning remains outside current scope.

## 4. Direction principles

1. **Family connection, not social-network growth.** The product optimizes trust, coordination, and usefulness—not engagement loops or public reach.
2. **Contact permission is different from content visibility.** A guardian controls who a child may contact; this does not automatically mean unlimited hidden reading of family/child conversations.
3. **Relationship context matters.** Family member, approved friend, relative, guardian, and emergency contact are different relationship types with different permissions.
4. **Coordination is a closed loop.** Event, task, check-in, request, and acknowledgement have ownership, time context, state, and recovery.
5. **Communication delivery is truthful.** Sent, queued, delivered, read, unavailable, failed, and unsupported are distinct states.
6. **Safety is always reachable where supported.** SOS and essential safety access stay visible as separate safety paths; normal communication capability is not an emergency guarantee.
7. **Children are respected.** Boundaries are explained, friend requests are transparent, and features do not expose a child to unknown people by default.
8. **Global family rhythm.** Calendar locale/time zone, translated content, RTL/LTR, accessibility, holidays, and optional Hijri presentation are configurable context, not assumptions for every family.

## 5. Required platform connections

| Connection activity | Required Family OS tie |
|---|---|
| Family message / call | Identity, roles, safe circle, notification state, activity/audit, emergency alternative. |
| Child friend request | Guardian approval, child explanation, safe-contact relationship, notification, audit. |
| Media/file share | Sender/recipient permissions, content lifecycle, storage/delivery truth, reporting/removal path. |
| Calendar event | Today, reminders, roles, time zone/calendar configuration, tasks/learning/focus context. |
| Family task | Child/guardian assignment, reward policy, learning/task link, notifications, activity history. |
| “I arrived” / location request | Location consent/freshness/capability, safety check-in, notification and response loop. |
| Call / group call | Contact policy, device capability, availability, history, safety fallback, support state. |

## 6. Scope boundary

### In scope

All 7 registered connection systems are active for refinement and will receive a V2 classification.

### Out of scope today

- Shopping, meal planning, recipes, broader household organisation extensions.
- Financial wallet, payments, allowance, or monetary chore payments.
- New ambient/audio monitoring capabilities.
- Public social network, open discovery, follower model, or unmoderated community chat.
- Third-party school, workplace, telco, or contact-book integrations not in the registered system universe.

## 7. Core journeys to close

1. Family member sends, receives, replies to, edits/deletes, and understands message delivery state.
2. Child requests an approved friend/contact; guardian reviews and the child sees a respectful outcome.
3. Parent or child starts a supported call and sees truthful availability/delivery/failure state.
4. Child shares an approved photo/file/voice note; the family sees appropriate delivery/status.
5. Guardian creates a calendar event that is visible to the correct members in their local time/calendar context.
6. Guardian assigns a task; child sees, completes, and confirms it; guardian responds or rewards it.
7. Child taps “I arrived”; designated guardians see check-in/location truth and can acknowledge.
8. Guardian requests location in a way that respects consent, freshness, support and safety boundaries.
