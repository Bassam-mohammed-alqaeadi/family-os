# Learning & Growth — Phase Initiation

> **Loop card:** LEARN-G1-DISCOVERY
> **Status:** Active; G1 direction accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Goal:** Turn Family OS learning into a connected growth system for the child, not a separate school portal or a collection of AI widgets.

## 1. Product outcome

Family OS Learning & Growth helps a child make meaningful progress every day while giving a guardian a simple, constructive understanding of how to support that progress.

The learning experience must answer:

| Person | Essential question |
|---|---|
| Child | “What is the next useful thing I can do, can I understand it, and can I feel proud of progress?” |
| Primary guardian | “Is my child progressing, where is help useful, and what is one supportive action I can take?” |
| Co-guardian | “What is assigned, completed, or needing support—and what may I change?” |
| Family platform | “How do learning, focus, calendar, tasks, time rules, rewards, and insight cooperate?” |

## 2. The Family Learning Loop

```text
Guardian chooses, creates, or approves a learning intention
    → child sees a clear next activity
    → child learns, practises, asks, or submits
    → learning state and result become visible
    → feedback explains progress and a next step
    → plan adapts where evidence supports it
    → effort is celebrated appropriately
    → guardian receives a concise support insight
    → focus/time/task/calendar systems remain coordinated
```

The loop is successful only when the child’s action, parent’s understanding, and platform context agree. A generated lesson, a quiz score, or a streak alone does not close the loop.

## 5. Scope boundary

### In scope for refinement

All 9 registered systems and their 65 services are included in product analysis. They will be classified as Core, Differentiator, Deferred Existing System, or Explicit Decision Required; registry priority/status/wave labels are not decisions.

### Out of scope today

- Expanded financial products, wallets, payments, allowance, or savings features.
- New external home/meal/shopping systems.
- New broad values/religion systems outside the registered education path.
- School district/student-information-system integrations not already in the system universe.
- Any public claim of real AI generation, camera/OCR, speech recognition, remote content import, or teacher-grade automation before it is implemented and verified.

## 6. Working learning principles

1. **Progress over activity.** Time spent is context, not proof of learning.
2. **Guidance over answer delivery.** A tutor helps a child reason, practise, and reflect; it does not become an answer machine.
3. **Child dignity over pressure.** Streaks, rewards, reports, and gaps motivate without shaming or public ranking by default.
4. **Guardian support over micromanagement.** Parents receive a practical next action, not an impossible stream of school data.
5. **Approved content lifecycle.** A source becomes child-visible only through clear provenance, suitability, assignment, and status.
6. **Adapt only from adequate evidence.** The platform says “not enough information yet” rather than fabricating a personalized plan.
7. **Global by design, culturally flexible.** Curriculum, language, age, learning style, RTL/LTR, Quran path, calendars, and time zones are modeled as configurable context.
8. **Focus is protected, not merely timed.** Security and learning cooperate so the child experiences a clear study environment with appropriate essentials.

## 7. Initial core journeys

1. Guardian sees a useful learning pulse for a child on Today.
2. Guardian creates or selects a lesson/assignment and assigns it with a clear due context.
3. Child sees the next learning action, completes it, and understands the result.
4. Child takes a formative assessment and receives constructive feedback.
5. Child asks the tutor for step-by-step help without receiving a direct answer.
6. Parent sees a learning gap or overdue work with one support action, not a guilt-inducing report.
7. Child starts a focus session; security/time rules cooperate and child understands the routine.
8. Child earns a non-financial achievement or permitted privilege from genuine progress.
9. Guardian reviews Quran progress; child follows a manageable memorization/recitation routine.
10. Guardian uses Parent Studio to create/approve/assign content without publishing unreviewed material to a child.
