# Family Intelligence Direction — Gate G1

> **Status:** Accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Purpose:** Classify the six intelligence systems and set a human-controlled, Render-governed intelligence direction.

## 1. Strategic recommendation

Build Family Intelligence as **Explainable Family Decision Support**:

> **Family OS turns real, authorized family events into understandable patterns and recommendations, then leaves meaningful decisions with the guardian.**

The product wins trust by explaining limits and uncertainty, not by pretending omniscience.

## 2. Six-system classification

| Intelligence system | Recommendation | Why | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signal engine | **Core intelligence foundation** | Source-qualified event/signal/risk representation enables all later insight. | No broad content/image monitoring claim until sources/models are proven. |
| Patterns & anomalies | **Capability-gated Differentiator** | Baselines and trends can create timely support after enough real data exists. | Sufficient data, confidence, explainability and correction required. |
| Family knowledge store | **Core governed foundation** | Unified timeline/context enables reports and assistant answers. | Purpose-limited data, source/visibility/forget/correction controls. |
| Advisor & reports | **Core basic + Differentiator advanced** | Weekly summary and one helpful next action can create value from verified events. | No fabricated report, automatic grading/generation/recitation conclusion. |
| Interactive assistant | **Capability-gated Differentiator** | Natural language can make complex family information accessible. | Role-aware retrieved data, source answer, “I do not know,” Render-only provider boundary. |
| Delegated agent | **Explicit Decision Required / later** | Valuable only after policy/command/audit/undo system is real and trusted. | Narrow delegation; no high-impact autonomous action by default. |

## 4. Role job maps

### Guardian

- Understand a signal/pattern with source, confidence and data limits.
- Choose support, dialogue, rule review, defer, dismiss or correct—without being forced into an automated response.
- Ask a question and receive a scoped evidence-backed answer or “I do not know.”
- Create only narrow delegation after seeing exact scope, expiry, actions, notification and undo semantics.

### Co-guardian

- Receives only analyses/action suggestions appropriate to their permission and relevance settings.
- Sees why an insight was surfaced and who acted; cannot gain broader family access through assistant queries.

### Child

- Sees supportive, age-appropriate explanation when an intelligence-derived plan affects them.
- Can ask for learning help within approved scope.
- Is never shown a hidden risk score, raw monitoring inference or stigmatizing label.

## 5. G2 Intelligence Hub hypothesis

```text
Family Intelligence Hub
 ├─ Today’s one useful insight
 ├─ Insights requiring guardian review
 ├─ Family / child timeline with source filters
 ├─ Weekly report and evidence
 ├─ Ask Family OS
 ├─ Data, memory and forget controls
 └─ Delegation centre (hidden until later capability is approved)
```

No “brain” screen becomes a dumping ground for all data. Intelligence stays embedded in the appropriate Safety, Learning and Family contexts, with a dedicated hub only for deeper explanation, control and review.
