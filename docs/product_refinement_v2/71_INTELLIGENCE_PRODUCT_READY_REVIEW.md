## 3. Integrity review

The pack preserves all active program decisions:

- Intelligence consumes source-qualified platform events and never makes a model output into a family fact.
- Production cannot use hard-coded family data/results/permissions/reports/insights or fake delivery/action success. Local/offline/test/demo fixtures must remain explicit and cannot become product truth.
- Render owns APIs, authorization, durable intelligence/family data, workers, audit and paid/usage-dependent workloads. Firebase is not a system of record and may only be used as approved no-cost auxiliary tooling.
- A guardian retains material decision authority; co-guardian scope remains explicit; child visibility is respectful and age-appropriate.
- “Not enough information,” partial, unavailable and “I do not know” are required trustworthy outcomes.
- No autonomous punitive, restrictive, contact, location, financial, reward, medical/legal/mental-health, emergency or otherwise high-impact decision is allowed.
- Product Ready records refinement completeness only. It does not convert an existing Flutter local/mock/in-memory/SQLite screen into a real AI or backend capability.
