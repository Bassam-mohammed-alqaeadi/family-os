# Administration, Trust & Operations Global Parity & Differentiation Matrix

> **Status:** Product-readiness evidence
> **Purpose:** Meet the family-platform expectations that make setup, membership, device state, notifications, privacy, subscription and support trustworthy—without copying vendor UX or making unsupported commercial/technical claims.
> **Research note:** Platform/store policy and pricing evolve. Account deletion, purchase verification/restoration, subscription state, notification delivery and regional terms must be rechecked at implementation and release time.

## 1. Outcome parity matrix

| Family outcome | Meaningful global expectation | Family OS direction | Truth boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account ownership and recovery | A person can access, secure, recover and eventually delete an account without losing clarity about active subscriptions/family responsibilities. | Account/recovery desk with verified state, limited diagnostics, account-deletion request and family-transition explanation. | Core; no login/recovery/deletion success before real identity/data lifecycle exists. |
| Family membership and roles | Adults join through invitations/approval, understand permissions and can recover safely; children do not self-grant control. | Primary guardian, configurable co-guardian and child scopes with invitation/acceptance/revocation/continuity/audit. | Core; client role selection is never authority. |
| Progressive first value | A family can learn product value without a forced maze and knows what setup remains. | Pliable Setup Queue with no-data/demo isolation, verified task state, pause/resume and repair route. | Core; UI progress is not a completion claim. |
| Device connection and health | A family sees which device is linked, whether it is current/limited and how to repair/revoke it. | Device Center with feature-specific capability/freshness, secure lifecycle, replacement/unlink and child explanation. | Core; device/natively enforced claim only after proof. |
| One daily family view | A busy guardian sees relevant actionable state rather than a product feature grid. | Today projects authorized source items, one priority, attention, people/day context and confirmed changes. | Core; routes to source owner and never invents family data. |
| Attention without overload | People expect channel/quiet/digest controls and appropriate urgent exceptions. | Role-scoped preferences, urgency/fatigue/time-zone policy, source reconciliation and explicit SOS boundary. | Core; push transport does not prove receipt or resolution. |
| Understandable privacy/data control | Users expect collection/visibility explanation, access/export/deletion pathways and activity history. | Purpose/source/visibility data map, child transparency, governed request status, retention explanation and least-privilege audit. | Core; no instant/global deletion claim without real propagation. |
| Honest subscription lifecycle | Families expect localized offer terms, trial/renewal/cancellation/restoration/refund clarity and verified benefits. | Render-verified entitlement after approved store/provider flow; clear pending/active/expiry/restore/refund states. | Capability-gated; no client-only purchase/price/receipt claim. |
| Accessible language and help | Families need localized, accessible controls and credible self-help/support status. | Arabic-first global localization/accessibility, current help content, consented diagnostics and real case lifecycle. | Core; no fictional agent/SLA/resolution. |
| Account deletion and data rights | Stores and users expect an in-app path to begin account deletion while required retention is explained. | Account/Data Request lifecycle starts in app, evaluates authority/retention/downstream impact, and reports completion/limits honestly. | Core design requirement; actual regional/legal implementation remains a later operating decision. |

## 2. Family OS differentiation

Family OS does not differentiate by hiding complicated administration under polished visual screens. Its defensible trust position is:

1. **One explicit family authority model.** Membership, child scope, device ownership, privacy, entitlement and support all use the same Render-authorized family context.
2. **Today with provenance.** The family front door is calm but each priority comes from an owning source with freshness, role and outcome truth—not from a generic dashboard score.
3. **Device health as family language.** A guardian sees exactly what is verified, limited, stale or repairable; a child gets a supportive explanation rather than unexplained enforcement.
4. **Trust before monetisation.** Billing has transparent verified lifecycle and never turns emergency, privacy/data access or recovery into a coercive upsell.
5. **Data dignity for every family member.** Guardians receive governed controls while children can understand active categories and request help without access to adult/private data.
6. **Recovery is part of the product.** Invitations, replacement devices, lost access, data requests, support and role changes retain auditable, humane paths instead of being dead ends.
7. **Transport honesty.** Notification and billing provider callbacks are operational signals, never a shortcut to conclude family delivery, action or entitlement without Render reconciliation.

## 3. Claims Family OS must not make before proof

- “Your family is fully set up,” “protected,” or “connected” from a local wizard/QR/demo/checklist alone.
- “This device is online/current/protected” without fresh supported device evidence for the claimed capability.
- “Your co-guardian has access” before the verified invitation/membership lifecycle is active.
- “You will receive every alert” or “we notified/read/solved it” based only on notification preference, queue or push attempt.
- “Your data is deleted/exported everywhere” from a local UI action or before retention/provider propagation completes.
- “Your subscription/trial/price/renewal/refund/restore is active” before provider/store and Render verification/reconciliation.
- “Free trial with no charge,” “cancel anytime,” “restored,” or equivalent without the exact current store/provider terms and lifecycle.
- “Support received/resolved your request” before the actual case system records that state.
- “AI settings are active” when the underlying Intelligence capability remains unavailable, local/mock or policy-gated.

## 4. Global readiness requirements

Before launch/marketing any administration slice, verify:

- regional account/identity, family/child/consent, privacy/deletion/retention and commercial policy obligations with appropriate specialist review;
- current Apple/Google/store/provider requirements for digital subscriptions, receipt verification, restoration, cancellation/refund, price/currency/tax disclosure and account deletion;
- a real server-side entitlement reconciliation path—purchase callbacks and client caches are not enough;
- platform-specific notification permission/background/offline/collapsed/out-of-order behavior, with in-app source reconciliation;
- Arabic-first RTL/LTR, language expansion, accessible text/input, screen-reader, calendar/time-zone/currency/terms and low-connectivity behavior;
- role, family, child, device and support access isolation across all APIs/workers/projections;
- retention, export/delete/forget/provider propagation, account-deletion and support operations with clear limitation language; and
- live help/support ownership, incident escalation and safe recovery for lost devices/accounts, privacy, billing and family authority conflicts.
