# Family Connection Direction — Gate G1

> **Status:** Accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Purpose:** Classify the 7 registered connection systems and establish the trust model for G2 UX.

## 1. Strategic recommendation

Build Family Connection as **Safe Family Coordination**:

> **Family members can communicate, coordinate time and responsibilities, share an approved moment, and check in safely—within understandable relationships and truthful delivery states.**

It is not a public social platform and it does not rely on hidden content surveillance to create safety.

## 2. Accepted trust model

1. **Family conversation is first.** The core value is reliable internal family communication and coordination.
2. **External contacts are opt-in relationships.** A child can request a friend; a guardian approves a known relationship and can later revise/block it.
3. **Contact control is not automatic content surveillance.** Who may communicate, what media is permitted, and who receives safety alerts are separate, explicit settings.
4. **Every delivery has a state.** Message, call, media, event, task and check-in use sent/queued/delivered/read/acknowledged/failed/limited states as appropriate.
5. **Calendar and tasks are core daily habits.** They are the coordination backbone connecting learning, safety, reminders, routines, and rewards.
6. **Location in communication starts with consented check-ins.** “I arrived,” request/respond, freshness and safety context precede any broad live-location promise.
7. **Calls are capability-gated.** In-app audio/video/group call claims wait for a real transport and platform capability model; urgent help uses the separate SOS/emergency pathway.

## 3. System classification

| System | Recommendation | Why | Conditions / boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversations | **Core, delivery-gated** | A family needs a dependable shared communication loop. | Relationship rules, transport, ordering, receipts, edit/delete rules, privacy/support model. |
| Calls | **Capability-gated Core commitment** | Direct family contact has high value, but a UI is not a calling service. | Real transport, permissions, availability, notifications, history, quality/failure and safety boundaries. |
| Media & files | **Core family sharing + advanced defer** | Photos/voice/files support connection, but need a lifecycle. | Capture/storage/delivery/rights/removal; transcription and decorative media are later. |
| Safe contact circle | **Core trust foundation** | External relationships require approval, block/revoke, child explanation and audit. | Relationship verification, invitations, age/consent, support/reporting. |
| Family calendar | **Core coordination loop** | Creates daily recurring family value and connects every pillar. | Real time-zone/calendar/prayer configuration, sync/conflict/reminders. |
| Tasks & responsibilities | **Core coordination loop** | Gives responsibilities a closed child/guardian confirmation and recognition loop. | Assignment/completion/review, non-financial reward/security linkage, recurrence/reminders. |
| Location in communication | **Capability-gated Core commitment** | Check-ins and arrival context create useful family reassurance. | Shared location consent/freshness/device/native delivery; no generic live-tracking claim. |

## 5. Role job maps

### Child

- Reach family through a clear, safe, supported channel.
- Understand who is approved, request a friend, and see the outcome respectfully.
- Share a moment only with the intended circle and understand delivery state.
- See assigned tasks/calendar events, confirm completion, and ask for help.
- Use “I arrived”/location request in a clear consented context.

### Primary guardian

- See family coordination priorities without reading every event or message.
- Configure safe relationships and revise/block them when needed.
- Create/revise events and responsibilities with clear ownership and reminder policy.
- Respond to a child check-in, request, or communication issue with real delivery evidence.

### Co-guardian

- Participate in the same family conversations/calendar/tasks within visible permissions.
- Know what was changed and by whom; avoid duplicate task/event/contact decisions.
- Respond to permitted requests/check-ins and escalate appropriate safety concerns.

## 6. Connection Hub hypothesis for G2

```text
Family Hub
 ├─ Today’s family coordination pulse
 ├─ Needs response: messages, requests, tasks, check-ins
 ├─ Family conversation and safe circle
 ├─ Today / week calendar
 ├─ Responsibilities and recent completions
 ├─ Check-ins and location-in-communication
 └─ Family connection settings
```

Family Hub is the parent’s connected front door. The child sees a simplified Home: conversations available to them, today’s events/tasks, approved friends, sharing, and check-in—with no adult administrative controls.
