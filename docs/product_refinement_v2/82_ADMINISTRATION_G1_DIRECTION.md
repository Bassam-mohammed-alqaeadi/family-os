# Administration, Trust & Operations Direction — Gate G1

> **Status:** Accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Purpose:** Classify the eight administration systems and set a family-trust, Render-governed direction for the platform spine.

## 1. Strategic recommendation

Build Administration as **Trustworthy Family Operations**:

> **Family OS helps a real family enter, connect, govern, understand and recover from its shared platform—with explicit authority, truthful state, respectful child transparency and no hidden commercial or technical surprise.**

This makes administration a practical daily trust layer, not a technical back office and not a set of static mock settings.

## 2. Eight-system classification

| Administration system | Recommendation | Why | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Initial setup | **Core trust foundation** | First-use clarity determines whether every later policy/device claim is understandable. | A wizard cannot claim account/family/device completion without remote verification. |
| Family and members | **Core trust foundation** | Membership, role, invitation and recovery authority underpin every product action. | Primary/co-guardian/child are explicit roles; legacy father/mother labels are not a hard-coded policy. |
| Devices | **Core capability foundation** | Device ownership/health/capability explains whether Safety/Learning/Connection can work. | QR/local UI is not pairing; no health/connection claim without verified device state. |
| Subscription and billing | **Core commercial foundation, capability-gated** | Clear entitlement/trial/renewal prevents billing from eroding family trust. | No purchase/receipt/restore/refund claim before supported store/payment and Render reconciliation exist; safety/data rights stay reachable. |
| Notifications | **Core delivery foundation** | Families need relevance, urgency, quiet and summary controls across all pillars. | Preferences/push attempts do not prove delivery/read/resolution; SOS has its own protected escalation policy. |
| Privacy and data | **Core governed foundation** | Consent, visibility, child transparency, retention, export/delete/forget and audit are necessary for responsible family products. | Local wipe/forget is not distributed deletion; legal/retention/provider outcomes must be explicit. |
| Today dashboard | **Core platform front door** | It converts the connected platform into one calm daily family view. | It aggregates real authorized source state only; no seeded pulse, fake suggestion or generic dashboard score. |
| Settings and support | **Core recovery foundation** | Findable control, localization, diagnosis and help keep complex family systems usable. | A help form/status label cannot imply human support, diagnosis or resolution until operationally real. |

## 4. Role job maps

### Primary guardian

- Establish/recover a family account and maintain membership/guardian continuity.
- Add a child and connect a device with clear consent, capability and repair state.
- See only real daily priorities and reach the owning safety/learning/family/intelligence workflow.
- Set notification, privacy, sharing and help preferences while understanding their actual effect.
- Manage a plan/renewal/restore/refund path without losing critical safety, privacy or recovery access.

### Co-guardian

- Join only through a verifiable invitation or recovery process with an explicit role/scope preview.
- See Today, notifications and settings within granted scope; know when a primary guardian must decide.
- Help repair a permitted device or support a child without silently changing authority, membership or privacy scope.
- See author/history for role, family, device, privacy and plan changes that affect their experience.

### Child

- Join/link a device with age-appropriate explanation of data, permissions, rules, visible controls and help path.
- See their own day/routine/device status in non-punitive language; report a problem or ask for help.
- Understand what data categories are enabled for them and when an adult changes a visible rule/device state.
- Never see billing, adult recovery, other members’ private settings, raw audit records or a false “connected/protected” claim.

## 5. Key direction decisions

| ID | Decision | Recommendation | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADM-G1-D01 | Role model | **Primary guardian + configurable co-guardian + child; invitation/recovery assigns roles.** | Replaces gendered/father-only implementation assumptions as V2 product law while allowing localized family labels. |
| ADM-G1-D02 | Setup posture | **Progressive and pausable; a clearly isolated no-data/demo route is allowed, but it cannot act as a connected family.** | No forced onboarding; no hard-coded fake dashboard, device or success state. |
| ADM-G1-D03 | Today posture | **Today is the role-aware, source-qualified family front door; domain systems retain ownership of action/outcome truth.** | Prevents an opaque all-purpose dashboard and fake intelligence/pulse content. |
| ADM-G1-D04 | Device posture | **Device pairing/health is a verified lifecycle with capability/permission/freshness/recovery—not a QR confirmation screen.** | Existing local QR/health UI becomes candidate UX, not evidence of secure enrollment. |
| ADM-G1-D05 | Notification posture | **Notifications are user-controlled attention transport with urgency/fatigue rules; critical/SOS escalation remains explicit and preferences never certify receipt.** | Render later owns recipient/relevance/orchestration; approved FCM could be a channel only. |
| ADM-G1-D06 | Privacy posture | **Purpose/visibility/child transparency/correction/export/delete/forget use one governed data lifecycle with honest propagation and retention state.** | Local lifecycle models are upgraded to Render contracts; no “delete everything instantly” promise. |
| ADM-G1-D07 | Commercial posture | **Subscription is a transparent entitlement lifecycle; trial, renewal, plan changes and restore/refund are capability-gated; critical safety, privacy and recovery are not coercive paywall tools.** | Payment/store integration remains later explicit capability work under Render authority. |
| ADM-G1-D08 | Support posture | **Settings and support are recoverable, accessible and honest: diagnosis/request/status only claim what exists.** | No fictional support agent, instant resolution, or hidden data access. |

## 6. G2 Trust Hub hypothesis

```text
Today
 ├─ Family pulse from real source items
 ├─ Needs my decision
 ├─ My children / active context
 ├─ Today’s schedule and responsibility context
 └─ Recent confirmed changes

Trust & Settings
 ├─ Family and members
 ├─ Devices and connection health
 ├─ Notifications and attention
 ├─ Privacy, data and audit
 ├─ Plan and subscription
 ├─ Language, accessibility and help
 └─ Account, recovery and support
```

Today does not duplicate the four domain hubs. It prioritizes and routes to them. Trust & Settings holds platform-wide control while each domain retains its specific settings desk.

## 7. Gate result

The direction classifies every registered administration system and reconciles all four unjourneyed administration services. The next loop will create the G2 UX lock: Today/Trust Hub information architecture, role journeys, setup/membership/device/commercial/privacy/notification state model and screen/state map. Backend, Native, provider, payment, notification transport and release work remain unauthorized.
