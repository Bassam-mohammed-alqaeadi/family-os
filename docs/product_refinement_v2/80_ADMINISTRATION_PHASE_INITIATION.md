# Administration, Trust & Operations — Phase Initiation

> **Loop card:** ADMIN-G1-DISCOVERY
> **Status:** Active; G1 direction accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Goal:** Make the shared family setup, identity, device, notification, privacy, Today, support and subscription spine trustworthy before any production implementation begins.

## 1. Product outcome

Administration is not a hidden settings folder. It is the platform’s trust operating system: the family can enter safely, understand who is in charge, connect real devices, control what is shared, see what matters today, recover when something fails, and understand commercial status without safety coercion.

| Person | Essential question |
|---|---|
| Primary guardian | “Can I set up and manage this family confidently, know what is real, and recover safely when something changes?” |
| Co-guardian | “What is my role, what can I see or change, and how can we avoid surprise or conflict?” |
| Child | “What is connected, what is shared, what rules affect me, and how can I ask for help or explain a problem?” |
| Family platform | “Can identity, device, consent, notification, privacy, Today and support truth remain consistent across every pillar?” |

## 2. Trust loop

```text
Account / family / membership intent
  → verified identity, role and recovery authority
  → child / device relationship and capability truth
  → appropriate policies, notifications and Today context
  → user action, delivery and independently verified outcome
  → privacy, audit, support and recovery record
```

No local screen, QR image, demo card, entitlement label, notification preference, audit row, device-health colour, or Today suggestion is proof of a remote family state, device pairing, payment, push delivery, policy effect, or support resolution.

## 4. Direction principles

1. **Membership before control.** A person’s family membership, role, recovery authority and child relationship determine every later view/action.
2. **Identity is not a gendered hard-code.** Existing father/mother surfaces are evidence only. V2 uses primary guardian and explicitly scoped co-guardian roles; an invitation assigns authority rather than a user selecting a powerful role for themselves.
3. **Setup creates trust, not forced completion.** A guardian may explore or pause setup, but every incomplete/unverified capability stays visibly limited.
4. **A device is a lifecycle, not a QR moment.** Pairing, permission health, capability, last verified connection, ownership, replacement, unlinking and recovery must remain visible.
5. **Today is a truthful prioritization layer.** It summarizes real, authorized platform events and links to their owning domain. It never invents a family pulse, recommendation or completed action.
6. **Notifications are transport and attention design.** Preference/priority/quiet time never replace the authoritative state, delivery receipt, SOS or domain outcome.
7. **Privacy is an active family control.** Purpose, scope, visibility, correction, export/delete/forget state and audit must be understandable; local deletion is not remote deletion.
8. **Commercial clarity protects trust.** Plan, trial, renewal, downgrade, restore and purchase state must be real, transparent and never make safety or data rights hostage to a payment screen.
9. **Support is a product recovery loop.** Help, diagnostics, status and request handling expose only permitted data and do not claim a human response or resolution before it exists.
10. **Render governs shared truth.** Any future account, family, membership, device, entitlement, notification, privacy, audit or support state is Render-authoritative. Firebase may only be an individually approved no-cost auxiliary service and never identity/family/product truth.

## 6. Scope boundary

### In scope

All eight registered administration systems are active for product refinement. They define shared platform contracts required by Security, Learning, Family Connection and Family Intelligence.

### Explicit boundary today

- No production authentication, email/social/phone login, account recovery, QR pairing, camera scan, device attestation, server membership, payment purchase, subscription receipt verification, FCM/push, remote export/delete, support ticketing, or status service is implemented by this refinement phase.
- A clearly labelled local demo or test fixture can help a person learn the UI, but cannot pretend to be their family, a connected child/device, current location, real notification, report, entitlement or completed onboarding.
- No role is permanently hard-coded to father/mother language or entitlement. The product supports culturally appropriate labels while preserving explicit, least-privilege primary/co-guardian authority.
- Financial responsibility products remain Future Development. Registered subscription and billing are commercial entitlement/service lifecycle only.

## 7. Core journeys to close

1. A guardian creates/rejoins a family or resumes setup without losing clarity about what is incomplete.
2. A guardian adds a child and links a device; both guardian and child understand permissions, data, capability and recovery.
3. A co-guardian receives, accepts or declines a scoped invitation and sees exactly what changed.
4. A guardian replaces, unlinks or repairs an unhealthy device without losing family/account safety or making false health claims.
5. A guardian opens Today and reaches the one real, relevant family priority without a simulated dashboard.
6. A person configures notification relevance/quiet hours and understands critical exceptions, delivery limits and alternatives.
7. A guardian reviews child/family privacy, corrects scope, requests export/delete/forget and sees honest propagation/retention state.
8. A guardian starts/changes/cancels/restores a plan with clear trial/renewal/pricing/receipt state while essential safety/data rights remain accessible.
9. A guardian or child changes language, finds help or submits a support request and sees request status rather than a fake response.

## 8. Edge cases that are mandatory, not polish

- Account recovery, lost guardian device, family-owner absence, co-guardian conflict, invitation expiry and role revocation.
- Child reaches age/consent threshold, family membership changes, or a guardian is removed/replaced.
- QR/device code expires, is scanned by the wrong account, device is already linked, replaced, factory-reset, offline or lacks required capabilities/permissions.
- Setup is paused, resumed on another device, abandoned, or shown only a safe no-data/demo preview.
- Daylight-saving/time-zone/travel changes quiet hours, scheduled digest or Today date context.
- Notification channel is disabled, push token changes, app is offline, duplicate events arrive, an alert is late, or a quiet rule conflicts with an emergency policy.
- A consent/privacy change races with an export, deletion, report, assistant retrieval or notification.
- Payment is declined, pending, refunded, restored, purchased on another platform, trial ends, plan changes, price/tax/currency differs, or store/server status conflicts.
- Support is unavailable, diagnostic upload fails, a person lacks authority, language/accessibility needs differ, or a help article is outdated.

## 9. G1 completion test

Gate G1 is complete when each of the eight systems has a defined family problem, affected roles, connected-platform responsibility, current source truth, major capability/commercial/trust uncertainty, V2 classification, service sequence, journey reconciliation and a reasoned product direction without starting Backend or Native work.
