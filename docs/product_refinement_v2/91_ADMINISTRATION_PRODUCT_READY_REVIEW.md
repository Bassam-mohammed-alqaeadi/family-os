## 3. Integrity review

The administration pack preserves the active programme rules:

- Production family/account/device/role/entitlement/notification/privacy/support/Today outcomes cannot be hard-coded, mock, local-only or falsely successful.
- Render will own authoritative APIs, authorization, durable family/product data, workers, audit, paid/usage-dependent workloads and backend-controlled integrations.
- Firebase cannot become a system of record. An FCM integration, if separately approved, is only a push transport attempt and cannot establish membership, delivery/read/resolution or product truth.
- Existing gender-specific Flutter father/mother code is inventory evidence only. V2 product authority is primary guardian, configurable co-guardian and child with explicit least-privilege scope.
- Demo/no-data, local cache and test fixtures are clearly separated from live family context and cannot trigger/represent real policy, device, report, alert, audit, entitlement or support result.
- Billing cannot coerce a family by withholding emergency/SOS, privacy/data rights or account recovery; purchase and provider client callbacks require server verification/reconciliation.
- Product Ready is a refinement state. It does not make existing local repositories, local SQLite/preferences, in-memory entitlement, mock QR/device health, static help, or Flutter UI into a real backend/native capability.
