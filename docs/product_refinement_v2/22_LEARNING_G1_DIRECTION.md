# Learning & Growth Direction — Gate G1

> **Status:** Accepted under standing Owner trust
> **Purpose:** Classify the 9 registered learning systems and establish the direction that G2 UX must realize.

## 1. Strategic recommendation

Build Family OS Learning & Growth as a **family learning loop**:

> **A child always has one meaningful next step; a guardian can support without becoming a full-time teacher; and the platform adapts only when real learning evidence supports it.**

This makes Family OS different from a content library, generic AI chat, or parental-control add-on. Education becomes a connected growth pillar that improves daily family life.

## 2. G1 direction accepted

The accepted product direction is:

1. **Core first:** lesson, assignment, assessment, focus, parent understanding, child progress, and safe non-financial encouragement.
2. **AI guides; it does not answer for the child.** Tutor, automatic feedback, generation, and adaptation are capability-gated and source-aware.
3. **Quran is an elective registered learning path.** It is rich where chosen by a family and does not become a forced global default.
4. **Rewards remain non-financial.** They may recognize progress or grant an explicitly permitted privilege; they do not become wallet, allowance, or payment products.
5. **Parent Studio is reviewed creation.** A guardian can create/approve/assign; an unreviewed generated or imported artifact never silently reaches a child.
6. **Advanced ingestion and community publishing wait for real capability and governance.** Camera/OCR, external links/files/voice, publishing, social competition, recitation correction, and rich AI generation do not make launch promises before proof.

## 3. System classification

| Learning system | Recommendation | Why | Conditions / boundaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subjects & lessons | **Core** | A child needs an understandable learning home and a guardian needs a clear lesson/content lifecycle. | Content provenance, age/level metadata, assignment state, local/offline truth. |
| Homework | **Core** | Creates daily learning follow-through and a concrete parent-child loop. | Due context, submission state, guardian visibility, notification and calendar/task coordination. |
| Tests & assessment | **Core baseline + Differentiator advanced** | Formative checks and understandable results are essential; auto-grading/generation require domain validity. | Child-friendly feedback, accommodation, question integrity, source-quality/assessment model. |
| Intelligent tutor | **Capability-gated Differentiator** | It can be transformative when it guides reasoning and is grounded in the child’s activity. | No answer machine, age/subject guardrails, conversation visibility rules, evaluation, provider/content truth. |
| Adaptive learning | **Differentiator** | Personalization becomes valuable after enough trustworthy results exist. | Skill graph, confidence, spaced review, meaningful gap detection, “not enough data” state. |
| Motivation & rewards | **Core non-financial loop + advanced defer** | Recognition supports habit and completion; it must not distort learning or become financial scope. | Effort/progress balance, child dignity, guardian policy, time-exception linkage, no default harmful ranking. |
| Quran & Islamic education | **Differentiator / elective registered path** | Existing depth can be a valuable learning experience when family-selected and respectful. | Optionality, progress over pressure, content accuracy, recitation capability truth. |
| Focus & study environment | **Core cross-pillar loop** | Connects learning intent with time, school mode, routine, and calm child experience. | Safety/time policy coordination, device truth, calendar/time-zone/sound capability. |
| Parent Studio | **Core reviewed assignment workflow + Differentiator advanced creation** | Guardian-created/approved learning is a distinctive Family OS advantage. | Manual/approved workflow first; ingestion/generation/publishing after lifecycle, safety, and capability proof. |

## 5. Role job maps

### Child

- See a small, motivating next learning action rather than an endless course catalog.
- Learn, practice, submit, receive feedback, and recover from mistakes without shame.
- Ask for conceptual help and receive guided steps rather than copied answers.
- Start focus, know the routine, and preserve essential access.
- Celebrate genuine effort and progress; understand rewards and their limits.
- Follow an optional Quran/recitation/adhkar routine in a respectful, manageable way.

### Primary guardian

- Understand what each child needs today in one glance.
- Assign/approve a focused activity rather than manage a complicated curriculum system.
- Notice an overdue item, difficulty, or success with a proportionate action.
- Create/approve content through Studio and know what was sent to a child.
- Configure focus and reward policies that work with security rather than fighting it.

### Co-guardian

- See relevant learning context and act within explicit permissions.
- Support, approve, or celebrate without creating duplicate/conflicting assignments.
- Understand who assigned/changed a learning plan and why.

## 6. Learning Hub hypothesis for G2

```text
Learning Hub
 ├─ Today’s learning pulse
 ├─ Per-child next step
 ├─ Needs guardian support
 ├─ Child progress and recent wins
 ├─ Focus now / next study routine
 ├─ Parent Studio and approved content
 ├─ Learning plans and reports
 └─ Optional Quran learning path
```

The hub connects to Today but does not duplicate it. Today surfaces the few family priorities; Learning provides the full growth context when a guardian chooses to go deeper.

## 7. G2 design questions already queued

- How does one child home balance “next action,” autonomy, joy, and overdue work without becoming a stressful school portal?
- How does a guardian see a learning gap without seeing raw surveillance or an opaque AI score?
- What does a reviewed content lifecycle look like from parent source to child completion?
- How do focus, time allowances, school mode, calendar, and task deadlines resolve conflicts visibly?
- How do Quran and learning paths remain optional, coherent, and culturally respectful?
- How do reward/streak/challenge mechanics celebrate progress without financial scope, unhealthy ranking, or pressure?
