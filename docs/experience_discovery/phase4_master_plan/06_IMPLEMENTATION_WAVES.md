## Wave laws

1. **Dependency-first** — not FS-001…010 numeric order.  
2. **Local before Native before Remote** unless product law says otherwise (SOS Local fire may precede remote delivery).  
3. **READY ≠ start** — READY only means candidacy after gates.  
4. **One authority** — no twin stores when converting.  
5. **Honesty badges** — NAT/REM MOCK states remain until that wave ships for real.
