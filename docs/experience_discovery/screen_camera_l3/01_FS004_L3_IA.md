## 1. UX principle

Screen & Camera Control is a **three-pillar protection system** with **honest capability claims**.

Parents configure **camera restriction**, **capture prevention** (where supportable), **screenshot monitoring** (transparent), and **Family OS surface protection**.  
Children see **transparency when monitoring is active** and truthful restriction/status — never admin.  
**Configured ≠ enforced** until ack + verified plane.

IA is organized by **role job**, not Stage-1 FAT-065/FAT-034 filenames.
